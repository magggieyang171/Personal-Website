Love Birds with Knives

By Maggie Yang

Some memories disappear. Some people never forget.

Sylvia’s life seems ordinary enough until she meets Solace, a strange, funny boy with soft hazel eyes, messy chestnut hair, and a knife he carries as casually as someone peeling an orange.

There’s something about him that feels familiar. His voice, his habits, the places they visit together. Even the smallest moments leave Sylvia with an inexplicable sense of déjà vu, as though she’s living a life she’s somehow forgotten.

As the two grow closer, Sylvia finds herself falling for Solace. Their days fill with laughter, late-night conversations, inside jokes, and moments that feel strangely like memories. But beneath their growing romance lies a mystery neither of them seems willing to confront.

Sylvia knows that something was taken from her. Her memories. Her past. Perhaps even a part of who she used to be.

And Solace knows more than he’s letting on.

As fragments of the past begin to surface, Sylvia must uncover the truth behind her missing memories and the boy who seems to know her better than she knows herself. But some truths are painful, and remembering everything may not be the same as getting everything back.

Because sometimes, the hardest part of love isn’t finding someone.
